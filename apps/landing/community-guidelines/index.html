Make room
for each other.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Share decks with friends or publish them for people browsing the marketplace. Help keep both spaces useful, respectful, and safe.

Share with care

  • Share learning content you created or have permission to distribute. Don’t copy paid or copyrighted material you aren’t allowed to share.
  • Publish only deck images and cards you have permission to distribute. Marketplace decks reach people beyond your friends, and downloaded copies remain in their libraries after you unpublish or delete your account.
  • Check generated suggestions before sharing. AI can make mistakes; a flashcard is not professional medical, legal, or financial advice.
  • Respect learning differences. Recall history is self-reported and should help a friend learn, not embarrass or pressure them.
  • Keep other people’s personal information, private messages, and confidential content out of shared cards and profile photos.
  • Respect a person’s choice to stop sharing or remove a connection. Don’t use new invitations to harass someone who has asked you to stop.

Content and behavior we don’t allow

  • Threats, targeted harassment, hateful attacks, or encouragement of violence.
  • Sexual exploitation, child sexual abuse material, or content that sexualizes minors.
  • Scams, impersonation, malware, or attempts to steal credentials or personal information.
  • Illegal content, instructions intended to facilitate serious harm, or material that violates another person’s rights.
  • Posting someone’s private information without permission, or repeatedly contacting them after they ask you to stop.

If something isn’t right

Open a person’s page in You → Friends, or open a marketplace deck, to report a concern or block the publisher. A report records the reason, optional details, and relevant deck content for private operator review; reporting does not automatically block the person. You can also use our support form and choose “Report shared content or a user.” Identify the relevant account or deck without including unrelated private information.

Blocking removes the connection, revokes shared-deck and detailed-progress access, and prevents new invitations or shares between the accounts. It also hides the blocked publisher’s marketplace listings and stops practice contributions between the accounts. Independent deck copies already accepted or downloaded stay in each person’s library. You can manage blocked people in Friends. Unblocking allows a new invitation; it does not restore the earlier friendship or sharing permissions.

Automatic text checks cover some abusive, exploitative, and threatening content before it is shared. Report harmful material that gets through, including profile images. Reports are reviewed privately by the operator; submitting a report does not publish it to other users or guarantee a particular outcome or response time.

We may restrict sharing, remove access to offending material, or suspend accounts to address violations, and may ask for more context about a report. Recollect support is not an emergency service; contact local emergency services if someone is in immediate danger.
