03 / GOOD QUESTIONSA FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING.

A little
more context.

What can I learn?+

Languages, science, art, music, or whatever you want to remember. Start with a Marketplace deck, generate a starting deck, or create every card yourself.

How does reviewing work?+

Tap a card to flip it. Swipe right when you remember, left when you’re unsure. Cards you’re unsure of return in a few days; cards you remember return after a week or longer. You can practice a deck whenever you like, and each swipe updates its review schedule.

Can I edit AI-generated cards?+

Yes. AI can make mistakes, so review and edit its suggestions before saving. Add your own cards, generate an answer draft, or ask for more suggestions in an existing deck.

Does it work offline?+

Once your library is on your phone, you can practice saved cards offline. You’ll need a connection to sign in, generate suggestions, download decks, and sync.

Recollect is currently in testing for iPhone. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you when access is available. There isn’t a public download link yet.
