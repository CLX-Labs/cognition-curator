04 / GOOD QUESTIONSA FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING.

Curious
about us?

What can I learn with Recollect?+

A language, an interesting subject, or a set of concepts you want to remember. Generate a starting deck or create every card yourself.

Can I add my own cards?+

Absolutely. Write your own question and answer, add a hint, and organize cards into decks. You can also generate an answer draft or get more suggestions for an existing deck.

How is practice different from daily reviews?+

Reviews show cards that are due. Practice lets you choose a deck and go through its cards whenever you want. In both modes, “I remember” and “Not sure” update the next review date.

Do I need an internet connection?+

You’ll need a connection to sign in, generate suggestions, and sync. After your library is on your phone, you can practice and review your saved cards offline.

Are AI-generated cards always right?+

No. Treat suggestions as a starting point. Review, choose, and edit generated cards before saving them, so you decide what goes into your deck.
