GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
you might wonder.

What can I learn with Recollect?+

Start with a language, an interesting subject, or a set of concepts you want to remember. You can generate a starting deck or create every card yourself.

Can I add my own cards?+

Absolutely. Write your own question and answer, add a hint, and organize cards into decks. You can also generate an answer draft or ask for more suggestions for an existing deck.

How is practice different from daily reviews?+

Daily reviews use your ratings to plan when a card comes back. Practice lets you go through a deck whenever you want, including another pass at uncertain cards, without changing your review schedule.

Do I need an internet connection?+

You’ll need a connection to sign in, generate suggestions, and sync. After your library is on your phone, you can practice and review your saved cards offline.

Are AI-generated cards always right?+

No. Treat suggestions as a starting point. Recollect lets you review, choose, and edit generated cards before saving them, so you stay in charge of what you learn.
