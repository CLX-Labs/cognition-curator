Recollect is provided by Collect Software. Email collectsoftwarev2@gmail.com or use the form below.

Before you write

  • App version: open You in Recollect and include the version or commit shown in app updates.
  • Sync or a stuck AI request: tell us which screen you’re on and any visible error. Keep your library on the device while we investigate.
  • Friend invitations: after installing, return to the original invitation or paste its code in You → Friends.
  • Marketplace decks: publishing shares a saved version beyond your friends. Open the deck’s marketplace settings to update or unpublish it, or to show or hide download counts and practice trends. Existing downloads stay in other people’s libraries.
  • Contributing practice trends: this is optional for each downloaded deck. Only eligible reviews after permission can contribute. Turn it off in the downloaded deck’s marketplace settings to remove your contribution from the trends.
  • AI permission: generation asks to share your question or topic, and up to 40 existing card questions when expanding a deck, with Anthropic. Change this permission in You. Requests already sent cannot be recalled.
  • Unwanted shared content: open the person’s page in You → Friends, or open a marketplace deck, to report a concern or block the publisher. Reporting and blocking are separate actions. See our community guidelines.
  • Privacy or account questions: choose “Account or privacy” below. Signing out leaves your account and library in place.

Don’t include passwords, Apple sign-in codes, invite codes, or private card contents you don’t want us to read.

Delete an account

Open You → Delete account. You can export your library first. Type DELETE and confirm with Apple to submit the request for that account.

An accepted request disables access. The app shows whether permanent server deletion is pending or complete and keeps a request reference for checking again. If a connection ends before confirmation, use the recovery screen to check or retry with Apple. Include the request reference when asking us for help.

After acceptance, the current account’s local library, drafts, profile cache, and widget data are removed from this device. Server deletion waits for Apple sign-in revocation and erasure of owned records, including your marketplace listings. Deck copies other people already accepted or downloaded, exports you saved elsewhere, and older native recovery files with unconfirmed ownership are separate. Contact us for help with older recovery files; don’t remove them while investigating a missing library.
